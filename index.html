About the Student Grievance Portal

An efficient, serverless web application engineered to digitize and streamline campus issue reporting for PDA College of Engineering.

⚙️ Technical Workflow & Architecture

  • Frontend Interface: Developed using lightweight, responsive HTML5 and CSS3 with a modern glassmorphic UI.
  • Serverless Backend: Utilizes Google Apps Script (`doPost` handler) as an intermediary webhook to capture form submissions.
  • Cloud Data Storage: Automatically logs incoming complaints directly into Google Sheets.

🌟 Operational Impact & Benefits

  • Departmental Segregation: Routes issues directly to ECE, CSE, Mechanical, Civil, or Administration.
  • Transparency: Replaces paper logs with digital timestamps and a live embedded tracker.
  • Academic Feasibility: Demonstrates full-stack integration suitable for undergraduate engineering projects.
